About us

Cheremshyna Mini-Hotel is located in the village of Velyatyno in the Zakarpattia region of Ukraine. It is a cozy place for a family holiday, relaxation and exploring the beautiful Zakarpattia countryside.

The main advantage of the location is its proximity to the "Tepli Vody" thermal complex, located only approximately 50 meters from the mini-hotel.

  • Comfortable Standard rooms;

  • Large parking area on the property;
  • Balcony or loggia;

  • Video surveillance;

  • Private enclosed territory.
